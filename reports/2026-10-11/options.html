Speculative Options Sleeve

Small, defined-risk option trades that express the Decision Desk's strongest trend calls, sized to a hard cap.

Read this first

  • Sleeve cap 5% of portfolio in total premium; at most 1% per trade. This money can go to zero.
  • Defined risk only: bought calls and puts, and debit spreads. No selling naked options, no margin.
  • Expect most trades to lose. The payoff comes from a few winners; size so a string of losses is affordable.
  • Exits are fixed in advance: take profit at +100% on long options (or 75% of a spread's maximum gain), cut at −50% of premium, close or roll with 21 days left.
  • Probabilities use the option market's own implied volatility. They show what the price assumes; the edge, if any, comes only from the trend call being right.

This week's sleeve

  • The cap fits 5 trades at full size. Take them from the top of the ranking, at most one per underlying.
  • Prefer spreads: they cost less, pay a defined amount and need a smaller move than single long options.
  • Skip any idea whose underlying has since crossed its Decision Desk exit level.

How to place one

  • Use a limit order at or below the mid price shown; never pay the full ask on a wide market.
  • For spreads, enter both legs as one combination order so you never hold one leg alone.
  • Set the exit alerts the same day: underlying exit level from the Decision Desk, −50% premium, and the 21-day time stop.
  • Log the trade in the Decision Book with the assumption, counter-case and pre-mortem.
