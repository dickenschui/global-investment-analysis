6 · Before you act: the five-question checklist

Every Accumulate decision passes all five before money moves. Record it in config/decision-log.csv.

  1. Assumption: write the one thing that must be true for this trade to work (e.g. 'AI capex keeps growing through 2027').
  2. Strongest counter-case: name the best argument against it, and what data would confirm it.
  3. Pre-mortem: it is six months later and the trade lost 20%. List the three most likely reasons, most probable first.
  4. Exit is pre-committed: the exit level in the Key level column is entered as an alert or stop before buying.
  5. Size within limits: amount ≤ the Max size column; the sector is not already over-weight; log the decision.
